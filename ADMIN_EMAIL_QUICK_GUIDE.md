# Email Messages Admin Quick Guide

## 📍 Where to Find It
In the Admin Panel sidebar, under **COMMUNICATIONS** section → **Email Messages**

## 🎯 Main Features

### 1. View All Emails
- **Page**: Email Messages (default index page)
- **Shows**: All emails sent from the platform
- **Info displayed**:
  - Recipient name & email
  - Email subject
  - Email type (Application, Enrollment, Booking, Manual)
  - Status (Sent, Pending, Failed)
  - Date sent
- **Actions**: Click any email to view full content

### 2. View Email Details
- **Page**: Click on any email in the list
- **Shows**:
  - Complete recipient information
  - Full subject line
  - Complete message body
  - Email type and status
  - Exact time sent

### 3. Send Custom Email
- **Page**: Click "Send Email" button
- **Steps**:
  1. Enter recipient(s) - one per line
  2. Select email type
  3. Write subject line
  4. Write message body
  5. Click "Send Email"

## 👥 Recipient Formats

You can enter recipients in these formats:
```
email@domain.com
Name <email@domain.com>
John Doe <john@domain.com>
```

## ⚡ Quick Recipient Groups

Use these buttons to auto-fill recipient lists:
- **Add All Users** - All registered users
- **Add Applicants** - People who applied for projects
- **Add Enrolled Users** - People enrolled in programs
- **Add Bookers** - People who booked services

## 📋 Email Types

When sending custom emails, choose the type:
- **manual** - Custom/promotional emails
- **application** - Project application related
- **enrollment** - Program enrollment related
- **booking** - Service booking related

## ✉️ Automatic Emails

These emails are sent automatically (no admin action needed):

### When someone applies for a project:
- ✅ Email sent to admin (with applicant details)
- ✅ Email sent to applicant (confirmation)

### When someone enrolls in a program:
- ✅ Email sent to admin (with enrollee details)
- ✅ Email sent to enrollee (enrollment confirmation)

### When someone books a service:
- ✅ Email sent to admin (with booking details)
- ✅ Email sent to booker (booking confirmation)

## 💡 Tips

1. **Always proofread** before clicking Send Email
2. **Test first** - Send to yourself before bulk sending
3. **Clear subject** - Make subject lines descriptive
4. **Professional tone** - Keep body text professional
5. **One batch** - Send all emails at once (don't resend)
6. **Check logs** - Review email history for records

## 🔍 Email Status Meanings

- **Sent** 🟢 - Email successfully delivered
- **Pending** 🟡 - Email queued for sending
- **Failed** 🔴 - Email failed to send (check logs)

## ❌ Troubleshooting

### Email won't send?
- Check all recipient emails are in correct format
- Make sure subject and body are not empty
- Try sending to just one recipient first

### Can't find Email Messages?
- Make sure you're logged in as admin
- Check admin sidebar - should be under COMMUNICATIONS
- Refresh the page

### Recipient list too long?
- Use bulk action buttons instead
- Paste emails line by line
- Remove invalid/duplicate emails
